How many hours do you need to drive with a learner's permit?

Most states ask for 40 to 60 hours of supervised driving, usually with 10 to 15 at night, and require holding the permit for 6 to 12 months. The table above lists the requirement in each state.

What counts as night driving?

Driving between sunset and sunrise. Hourglass counts it automatically from the real sunset and sunrise where each drive starts.

Does the DMV accept a phone log?

Many states and driving schools accept a parent-signed log; some require their own form. Hourglass exports a PDF that makes either easy.

Is anything I type sent anywhere?

No. The calculator runs in your browser and the page cannot connect to any server.
