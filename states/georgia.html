How many hours do you need to drive with a learner's permit in Georgia?

Georgia asks for 40 hours of supervised driving, 6 of them at night, and at least 12 months holding the permit before the road test.

How many hours a week is that?

Spread over 12 months, 40 hours is about 0.8 hours a week. The calculator above adjusts the pace to the hours already driven and your test date.

What counts as night driving?

Driving between sunset and sunrise. Hourglass counts it automatically from the real sunset and sunrise where each drive starts.

Where do I check the official Georgia rules?

On the Georgia DMV (or Department of Public Safety) website. Requirements change, and some states lower the hours after driver education.
