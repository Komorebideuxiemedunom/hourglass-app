Frequent questions

Where is my log stored?

On your iPhone, and in its iCloud backup. Hourglass has no server.

I changed phone

Restore your iPhone from its iCloud backup: your log comes back with it. Your Hourglass Pro purchase is restored from Settings › Restore purchase.

How is night time counted?

From sunset to sunrise where the drive started (9 PM to 6 AM if location is off). You can edit it on any drive.

Is the PDF accepted by my DMV?

Many states and driving schools accept a parent-signed log; some require their own form. Check yours: the PDF makes copying easy.
